The premise

Close the loop from filing to funding.

Fundir reads foundation filings to find funders worth pursuing, and indexes your own reports and metrics so you can answer any application with what you have already documented. Both sit in one system, so every prospect is measured against what your organization can actually show.

In-house data hub

Everything your team already knows, in one place.

Program reports, outcome workbooks, past narratives, board documents. Fundir indexes what the organization has written and lets anyone on the team ask for it in plain language.

Ask for the metric, not the folder.

An assistant reads the corpus your team uploads and answers with the passage it came from. Ask which outcomes fit an application's requirements, or which numbers belong in this quarter's report, and get the source alongside the answer.
